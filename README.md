# Calvinist Parrot

Calvinist Parrot is a free theological assistant rooted in the Reformed tradition. Ask a question about Scripture, explore how Christian traditions understand a doctrine, or find a church near you.

[Try Parrot in your browser](https://www.calvinistparrot.com/) · [Connect your AI client](https://www.calvinistparrot.com/connect) · [Changelog](./CHANGELOG.md)

Calvinist Parrot Ministries exists to glorify God by creating and freely distributing AI tools that proclaim the Gospel, strengthen the Church, and equip believers across the globe. [About the ministry](https://www.calvinistparrotministries.org/)

## Try Parrot

- **Ask a theological question.** Try “What does Paul mean by grace in Ephesians 2:8–10?” Follow the Scripture references in the answer or ask a follow-up. You can explore Reformed Baptist, Presbyterian, Wesleyan, Lutheran, Anglican, Pentecostal/Charismatic, and Non-Denominational Evangelical perspectives. [Open chat](https://www.calvinistparrot.com/)
- **Find a church.** Search by location and read a saved doctrinal evaluation alongside its supporting sources. [Church Finder](https://www.calvinistparrot.com/church-finder)
- **Read and reflect.** Read a devotional connecting Scripture with current events. Write a personal journal reflection, explore it in chat, or turn it into a prayer request. [Daily Devotionals](https://www.calvinistparrot.com/devotional) · [Personal Journal](https://www.calvinistparrot.com/journal)
- **Practice faith as a family.** Plan discipleship with your children and record everyday parenting moments. Keep track of personal and household prayer requests and rotate whom you pray for. [Heritage Journal](https://www.calvinistparrot.com/kids-discipleship) · [Prayer Tracker](https://www.calvinistparrot.com/prayer-tracker)
- **See how the models are evaluated.** Compare published results for doctrinal fidelity, kindness and gentleness, and interfaith sensitivity. [Public AI Model Evaluation Dashboard](https://www.calvinistparrot.com/llm-evaluation-dashboard)

## Connect from another app

The hosted Model Context Protocol (MCP) server lets compatible clients search for churches and read saved evaluations. Clients that allow write actions can also ask a guest question or, with your permission, save and continue a conversation in your Parrot account. The setup guide covers ChatGPT, Codex, and Claude, including permissions and how to disconnect. [Connect to Parrot](https://www.calvinistparrot.com/connect)

If you are building an integration, the public API reference describes requests, responses, and streaming events. The runnable Node.js example shows how to compare churches across cities. [API reference](https://www.calvinistparrot.com/api/v1/docs) · [OpenAPI contract](https://www.calvinistparrot.com/api/v1/openapi.json) · [Church comparison example](./scripts/examples/compare-churches.mjs)
